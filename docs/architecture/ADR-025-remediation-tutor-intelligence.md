# ADR-025 — Remediation Intelligence & Tutor Engine

## Decision
EduNova treats a misconception as a probabilistic learning hypothesis, never as a psychological or medical diagnosis. The tutor records evidence, confidence and root-cause category, then selects a deterministic, explainable intervention.

Mastery cannot be restored by explanation or repeated exposure alone. Resolution requires independent reassessment plus transfer to a novel context. Hint usage is evidence, not success. After resolution, spaced retention checks can detect relapse and reopen remediation.

High-stakes explanations remain source-bound to editorially validated content. Generative providers may draft pedagogical wording but cannot establish scientific truth or silently change the reference answer.
