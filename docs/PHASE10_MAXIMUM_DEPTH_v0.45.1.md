# EduNova v0.45.1 — Phase 10 Maximum Depth

Phase 10 now covers both institutional placements and supervised academic research. Research projects are scoped to an active student registration, typed as TFC/memoire/these, supervised by an active institutional member, versioned, and governed through controlled states. Students submit immutable revision references with SHA-256 evidence; supervisors/officers review each revision. Defense readiness requires an approved latest revision. Defenses enforce chair/secretary separation and persist a SHA-256 seal of the minutes text. These hashes are integrity evidence, not qualified electronic signatures.

Placement foundation from v0.45.0 remains intact. Provider/device/storage certification remains outside this backend checkpoint.
