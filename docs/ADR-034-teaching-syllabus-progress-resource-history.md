# ADR-034 — Teaching delivery as an auditable academic record

EduNova v0.24.1 makes the institutional syllabus a versioned aggregate. Learning outcomes and ordered topics are explicit, topics may reference Knowledge Graph concepts, and progression is recorded against an approved syllabus. Resource changes create immutable revision records and return the mutable resource to draft before republication. Approval is limited to institutional administrators or the lead teacher. Intelligence may propose structure, but never silently approves institutional truth.

Boundaries remain strict: assignments/submissions belong to LOT24; official gradebook/results belong to LOT25.
