# EduNova v0.40.1 — Phase 5 Maximum Depth

This hardening pass adds governed identity corrections with before/after evidence, reasoned academic-registration lifecycle transitions, optimistic locking, terminal-state protection, and progression guards preventing a new academic-year registration while the previous one remains open or when academic years regress chronologically. Administrative history is institution-scoped and append-only at the application layer.

Certification boundary: backend/static tests in this environment; Flutter/device and production PostgreSQL concurrency remain machine-certification work.
