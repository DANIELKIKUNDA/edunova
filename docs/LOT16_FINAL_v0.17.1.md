# LOT 16 FINAL — Student Intelligence & Adaptive Learning v0.17.1

## Final scope
Adaptive-v2 turns LOT 15 evidence into explainable guidance without claiming pedagogical certainty. It adds evidence confidence, cross-signal knowledge gaps, exam readiness, at-risk course detection, prioritized actions, snapshot history/trends, and a premium student intelligence surface.

## Safety and pedagogy
- Activity is never equated with mastery.
- Missing evidence lowers confidence instead of inventing certainty.
- Every recommendation carries evidence, reason, confidence, urgency and expected outcome.
- Exam readiness is an operational study indicator, not a prediction of grades or academic success.
- LLMs are not authoritative for mastery state.

## Certification boundary
Backend, migration contract, compileall and structural audit are certifiable in ChatGPT. Flutter builds, device UX, PostgreSQL concurrency, offline multi-device and load/endurance remain OPEN-machine.
